I don't think of design and development as separate jobs — they're one continuous decision about how something should work.

My journey into software started with a deep curiosity for how digital tools work under the hood. Since then, I've spend my time diving into full-stack development - bridging the gap between robust back-end logic and responsive front-end interfaces.

What I Focus On

When building web projects, three principles guide my process:

  • Clean Architecture: Writing modular, readable, and maintainable code that scales smoothly.

  • Performance & UX: Optimizing assets, reducing render time, and ensuring interfaces feel fluid across all devices.

  • Continuous Growth: Exploring new frameworks and tooling to keep expanding what I can build.
